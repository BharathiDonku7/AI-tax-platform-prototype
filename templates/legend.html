Rules the system follows

  • 01 State is never encoded by color alone. Every state carries a border, a chip, and a text label, so the system survives color blindness and grayscale printing — which matters when a return package gets printed for a client meeting.
  • 02 Locked always explains itself. A disabled field with no reason is the most common source of support tickets. Every locked field carries a sentence telling the user what to change instead.
  • 03 Confidence is banded, not decimal. The panel leads with "Low confidence," not "0.61." A raw score implies a precision the model doesn't have, and CPAs will anchor on it.
  • 04 Correcting the AI never erases it. The original suggestion, the correction, the reason, and the reviewer stay in the record. Under Sec. 280E audit scrutiny, that history is the substantiation.
  • 05 Derived values show their chain, not a number. Clicking a calculated field replaces the document viewer with its full derivation, each step linked to its own source.
